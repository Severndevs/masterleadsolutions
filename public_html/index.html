About Us

Meet Shimon Hanfling

As the founder of Master Lead Solutions, Shimon brings over 3 years of hands-on residential assessment experience — honed through comprehensive on-site inspections for window and door replacement projects across Cleveland's older housing stock.

Recognizing the critical need for qualified lead hazard professionals in the area, Shimon completed both the Ohio Lead Inspector and Ohio Lead Risk Assessor training programs, as well as the EPA Renovation, Repair, and Painting (RRP) certification. His background in identifying structural decay and paint deterioration makes him uniquely qualified to detect lead hazards others might miss.

Master Lead Solutions is committed to a single mission: keeping Cleveland-area families safe in their own homes.
